Alpha was once naturally habitable, until industrial pollution, the first Grey Rain and a worsening climate forced a full evacuation. Centuries later, a fragmentary treasure map leads Traveller toward a sealed freight node beneath C-3—until an acid-rain crash landing turns the surviving piers of The Spine into the only line through a vanished road.

OOO Studio《深空接收机》获SheNicest千人黑客松游戏赛道一等奖

2026年8月30日，SheNicest 2026「烈变」夏季千人黑客松在北京国家会议中心落幕。独立游戏团队OOO Studio凭借项目《深空接收机》，获得游戏开发赛道一等奖。

本届黑客松为期五天，汇聚近千名创作者。参赛者围绕软件应用、智能硬件、游戏开发、AIGC影像和量子计算五大赛道，在96小时内完成创意验证、产品开发与现场路演。

《深空接收机》是一款桌面端复古科幻体验，试图重新设计AI生成、代码构建和渲染过程中常被忽略的等待时间。当新的宇宙信号到来，用户可以暂时离开工作，在1至10分钟内探索一个未知文明，并通过图片、影像和可旋转3D物件，逐步拼合它的历史。所有发现都可以被收藏与归档，最终形成玩家个人的深空探索档案。

与强调持续参与和即时奖励的传统游戏不同，《深空接收机》选择安静地留在后台。它不要求用户持续关注，不制造压力，也不会因错过信号而惩罚用户。相反，这个项目希望把原本零散的时间，转化为短暂、完整且值得期待的远征。

OOO Studio是一支由五人组成的独立团队，成员分布于英国、瑞典、澳大利亚、中国大陆和香港，拥有游戏设计、软件开发、技术美术、AIGC艺术和增强现实开发等领域的经验。

获得一等奖，是这支跨越国界和时区协作的团队的重要里程碑。黑客松结束后，OOO Studio将继续完善项目的交互设计、内容生产流程和虚构世界观，目标是把《深空接收机》从一个获奖原型，发展为一款能够持续成长的独立游戏作品。

每一次等待，都是一次远征。

OOO Studio’s Deep Space Receiver Wins First Prize at the SheNicest Hackathon

On August 30, 2026, the SheNicest “FISSION” Summer Hackathon concluded at the China National Convention Center in Beijing. Independent game team OOO Studio received First Prize in the Game Development Track for its project, Deep Space Receiver.

The five-day event brought together nearly 1,000 creators working across five tracks: software applications, intelligent hardware, game development, AIGC film and quantum computing. Participants developed and presented functional prototypes during an intensive 96-hour creative sprint.

Deep Space Receiver is a retro-futuristic desktop experience designed to reimagine the idle moments created by AI generation, code compilation and rendering. When a transmission arrives, users can step away from work and spend between one and ten minutes exploring an unknown civilization. Images, video transmissions and interactive 3D artifacts gradually reveal fragments of its history. Each discovery can be collected and archived, allowing players to build a personal record of their journeys into deep space.

Unlike conventional games built around constant engagement and immediate rewards, Deep Space Receiver is designed to remain quietly in the background. It does not demand attention, create pressure or penalize users for missing a transmission. Instead, the project turns otherwise disposable fragments of time into short, self-contained expeditions worth anticipating.

OOO Studio is a five-person independent team distributed across the United Kingdom, Sweden, Australia, mainland China and Hong Kong. Its members bring together experience in game design, software development, technical art, AIGC art and augmented reality development.

Winning First Prize marks an important milestone for a team collaborating across borders and time zones. Following the hackathon, OOO Studio will continue developing the project’s interaction design, content pipeline and fictional universe, with the goal of transforming Deep Space Receiver from an award-winning prototype into an independent title capable of growing over time.

Every wait is an expedition.
